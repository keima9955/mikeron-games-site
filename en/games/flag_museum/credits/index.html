Flag Museum: Sources

このページは英語のみです。主な出典は説明書の「データの由来」をご覧ください。

This page lists everything behind the numbers, maps, flags and icons printed on the 155 cards of Flag Museum: the datasets, books and articles (in APA style), the author and license of every flag image, and the Wikipedia articles from which individual values were taken. The rulebook's "Data Notes" explain how the numbers were made. Last updated 2026-11-03.
